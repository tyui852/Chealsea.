Welcome to Chelsea Football Club

Chelsea Football Club, founded in 1905, is a professional football club based in London, England. With a long and successful history, Chelsea has won numerous major domestic and international championships. Whether in the Premier League or European competitions, Chelsea remains a top-tier team.

Club history

Chelsea's history dates back to 1905, when they founded the club at Stamford Bridge. Since then, the club has grown into a world-class team, winning numerous domestic and international competitions.

club trophies

Premier League — 6 titles 1954–55, 2004–05, 2005–06, 2009–10, 2014–15, 2016–17

Second Division — 2 titles 1983–84, 1988–89

FA Cup — 8 titles 1969–70, 1996–97, 1999–2000, 2006–07, 2008–09, 2009–10, 2011–12, 2017–18

UEFA Champions League — 2 titles 2011–12, 2020–21

UEFA Europa League — 2 titles 2012–13, 2018–19

UEFA Super Cup — 2 titles 1998, 2021

UEFA Europa Conference League — 1 title 2024–25

FIFA Club World Cup — 2 titles 2021, 2025
